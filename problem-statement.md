# Problem Statement

In many Indian cities, students are constantly looking for flexible ways to earn money while studying.

Currently, most gig opportunities are limited to platforms like food delivery or ride sharing. However, there are thousands of small everyday tasks that people need help with, but there is no simple platform connecting these needs with people who are willing to do them.

Examples of such tasks include:

- Grocery purchasing
- Flat, hotel, or car cleaning
- Moving assistance
- Furniture mounting
- Basic home assistance
- Small errands

At the same time, many students are actively searching for short-term work opportunities that do not require long-term commitments.

Despite the demand on both sides, there is no simple local platform that connects people who need small tasks done with students who are willing to complete them.

This creates a clear market gap.

## Proposed Solution

WorkBee is a platform designed to connect people who need small tasks completed with students looking for flexible earning opportunities.

The platform allows users to:
- Post small tasks
- Allow nearby workers (students) to accept tasks
- Track task progress
- Complete payments securely

By bridging this gap, WorkBee creates a simple micro-task marketplace within cities.
